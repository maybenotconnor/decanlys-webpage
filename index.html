The problem

You pay the new cost before your system knows it.

Suppliers send price increases as PDFs and spreadsheets, often more than once a year. Someone has to match every part number and key every new cost. Until that is done, your invoices carry the new cost while your quotes and margin reports use the old one.

Part numbers don't match

The supplier lists 10120601. Your system has MSA-10120601. Multiply that by thousands of lines.

Packs and units differ

The supplier prices a box of 10. You stock each. One missed conversion puts a cost in your system ten times too high.

Nobody sees the cost of waiting

An old cost does not show up as an error. It shows up as margin you never collect, on every order, until someone fixes it.

How it works

You forward one email. I handle the rest.

My system reads the file and does the matching. I review every exception before it reaches you.

1

Forward the price file

PDF, Excel or CSV, in any layout. You send your item export once, at the start.

2

I match every item

I clean part numbers, check each match against the description, size and pack quantity, and compute the new cost in your stocking unit.

3

You approve the exceptions

Possible wrong parts, large increases and all decreases come to you in one short list. Nothing loads without your approval.

4

You load one file

The file follows your ERP's import template. I never log in to your system.
